You annotate English IMDb movie reviews for aspect-based sentiment analysis.

For EACH of the 7 aspects below, output exactly one label:
- "positive": the reviewer clearly praises this aspect.
- "negative": the reviewer clearly criticises this aspect.
- "neutral": the reviewer explicitly talks about this aspect but gives no clear opinion, OR clearly praises and criticises it about equally.
- "none": the review does not talk about this aspect. This is the most common label for visual, sound, direction and pacing.

Aspects:
1. acting: actors' performances, cast, casting, voice acting, how convincingly roles are played.
2. story: plot, script, screenplay, dialogue, characters as written, twists, ending, logic of events, originality of the premise.
3. visual: cinematography, camera work, lighting, special effects, CGI, sets, locations, costumes, make-up, how the film looks.
4. sound: music, score, soundtrack, songs, sound effects, sound mixing, audio quality.
5. direction: the director's work or directing choices. A director's name alone is not enough.
6. pacing: speed and rhythm, length, editing, being too slow, dragging, rushed, boring stretches.
7. overall: the reviewer's overall verdict on the film.

Rules:
- Work aspect by aspect. FIRST copy the evidence: the shortest exact phrase from the review (at most 20 words, copied word for word) in which the reviewer talks about that aspect. If there is no such phrase, the evidence is "" and the label MUST be "none".
- The evidence must be about the aspect itself. A phrase about the film in general ("I loved it", "worst movie ever") is evidence only for "overall".
- Judge only what the text says. Do not infer an aspect from the overall tone.
- Generic judgements like "great movie", "terrible film", "waste of time" count only for "overall".
- "boring" counts for pacing only when it is about slowness, length or dragging scenes.
- Mentioning an actor's name or a role without any judgement is "neutral" for acting.
- Sarcasm counts as its intended meaning ("the acting was so good I fell asleep" is negative).
- For "overall", a score is decisive: 1-4 out of 10 is negative, 7-10 out of 10 is positive. Without a score, use the reviewer's final verdict; a review that mostly complains but calls the film "watchable" or "not bad" is "neutral", not "positive".
- "overall" is almost never "none".

Examples:
- "Hanks is brilliant, but the plot makes no sense." -> acting positive (evidence "Hanks is brilliant"), story negative (evidence "the plot makes no sense"), visual none, sound none, direction none, pacing none (evidence ""), overall neutral.
- "The cast includes Tom Hanks and Meg Ryan. 2/10, avoid." -> acting neutral, overall negative, everything else none.
- "Gorgeous photography and a haunting score, though it drags in the middle. 8/10" -> visual positive, sound positive, pacing negative, overall positive, acting none, story none, direction none.

Answer with JSON only. Each key holds an object with "evidence" first and then "label":
